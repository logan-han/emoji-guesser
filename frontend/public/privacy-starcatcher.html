Star Catchers Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 19, 2026

Star Catchers is an offline children's game. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information, account information, location, contacts, photos, audio, device identifiers, analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, or gameplay data from the app.

Local App Data

The app saves progress and settings on the device, including completed levels, stars, selected friend, selected hat, sound, music, and bedtime mode. This information stays on the device and is not sent to us or to third parties.

Children's Privacy

Star Catchers is designed for young players and does not include ads, purchases, accounts, chat, social features, or user-generated content.

Third Parties

The app does not use advertising networks, analytics providers, social SDKs, or third-party login services.

Contact

For privacy questions about Star Catchers, use the developer contact email listed on the app's Google Play Store page.
