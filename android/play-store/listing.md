## Full description (max 4000 chars)
Emoji Guesser is a quick, social party game where one player describes a secret word using only emojis while the others race to guess it before the clock runs out. Real-time multiplayer over the web and on Android: play with friends in a private room or jump into a public lobby.

### How it works
- The describer picks a word from three options.
- They send emojis one at a time. No letters, no hints from them, just emoji.
- Everyone else types their guess. The first correct guess wins points for both the guesser and the describer.
- A new player takes the describer chair each round. Highest score after every player has gone once wins.

### Features
- Quick rounds (30 to 300 second timer per turn).
- Private lobbies with shareable game codes.
- Public lobbies if you want to play with strangers.
- Sound effects and haptic feedback you can toggle off.
- Adaptive light and dark themes.
- Free, no ads, no accounts.

Play head-to-head with your group chat, your family on a road trip, or your team on a long Zoom call. Built for short, repeatable rounds that work as well in 30 seconds as 30 minutes.
